SOW / PWS Builder

Make the scope decisions; the tool assembles a structured draft Statement of Work or Performance Work Statement — with citations that resolve to either the codified FAR or the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul, an EO 14402 fixed-price gate, and staffing and CLIN data kept out of the requirement body by design.

This tool produces a structured first draft to accelerate your requirement. It is a drafting aid, not a finished document or legal advice. A contracting officer must review, complete, and validate every section before use.

Before you start — read this:
  • This is an independent drafting aid, not an official Government system and not legal or contracting advice.
  • It produces a draft with marked placeholders. A contracting officer must review and complete it.
  • Regulatory references are current as of July 2026 and the RFO model text moves often — verify against acquisition.gov/far-overhaul before any solicitation release.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored on a server, or transmitted anywhere.
